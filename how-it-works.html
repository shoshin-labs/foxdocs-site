How it works

The sequence, in order.

This is the path through the app. Reviews sit after the overhaul and the plan, and they keep coming back.

01

Import the document

Upload what you have. The original stays in the register.

02

Overhaul it

Run a full rewrite when the current text is not fit for purpose.

03

Create an improvement plan

Scope the remaining work. Accept or reject each proposal.

04

Execute the plan

The accepted changes land as a new version, not a silent patch.

05

Review it on a cycle

Periodic reviews check compliance. Every review cuts another version.

06

Publish

Sign off the version you want live. Timeline and activity stay attached.

07

Distribute to the team

Send it to employees. Track who acknowledged it.
