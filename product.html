The register, not a chat window

Named reviewers. Plans you approve. A file you can defend.

Foxdocs is a policy and compliance document platform. The app is at app.foxdocs.com, and it is in BETA. Org switcher and dark mode live there. This page is the public map of what it does.

Reviews

Four sub-agents, not one vague score.

Integrity first. Language second.

Start Analysis runs a review workflow. Organisation settings let you pick which sub-agents are on by default. They sit in two groups.

Fidelity

Facts and law. Factual accuracy and legal compliance.

Completeness

Gaps and structure. Missing information or incomplete sections.

Mechanics

Language, spelling, and readability.

Style

Formatting, tone, and consistency with the organisation.

The document keeps a score (for example 8.9/10). That mark is a reading of this draft, not a trophy for the company.

Improvement plans

Scope it. Then accept or reject, line by line.

Improve document generates a structured plan before any change is applied. You set the scope. Proposals come back as discrete edits, with a reason, often tied to a source or a duty.

Accept the ones that belong. Reject the rest. Applied proposals save as a new version. Remaining ideas from an older plan do not sneak onto the new version. You start a fresh plan if you want another pass.

Overhauls

When the draft is too far gone.

Overhaul preserves your content and reorganises the document against its document type template. Use it when you need a full rewrite, not another handful of proposals.

Overhauls, Improvement Plans, and Reviews sit under Documents in the app. They are three tools on the same file, not three products.

Playbooks

A procedure people can actually follow.

Playbooks are a flowchart builder. A policy can say what should happen. A playbook shows the forks: who is called, what is recorded, when it escalates.

Publish a playbook when the steps are ready. It lives next to the register, not inside a slide deck.

Knowledge

Crawl the sources you already trust.

Knowledge is URL crawl and sync. You create a source, add URLs, and keep it active. Reviews can then work against a custom knowledge base, not a generic guess.

Business includes a custom knowledge base and regulation horizon scanning. Policy Co-Pilot sits on that same stack, for staff who need the policy in the moment.

Staff and seats

Employees are not users.

Users

People who sign in to the app. Seats. They run reviews, plans, overhauls, and publish.

Employees

The staff register. Name, email, position, alerts. Import CSV or add one by one. They receive distributions and acknowledge policies. They do not need a seat.

Growth includes 5 seats and 30 staff. Business includes 10 seats and 150 staff. Enterprise is unlimited, by arrangement. See pricing.

Publish, classify, export

A version you can put on a file.

Each document has tabs: Content, Activity, Versions, Timeline, Distributions, Shares, Export.

Publish a version. Classification sits on that published copy. Shares cover people you invite. Export is the branded pack for audit: Excel for a detailed change report (version history, section-level changes, approval status) and PDF for an executive summary with the version timeline.

Chat to Edit is there when you want a focused editing conversation on this version, without leaving the document.
