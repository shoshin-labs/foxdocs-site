Example · not a real customer

Harbor Care imports one file. Four homes get the published version.

A labelled walkthrough of the real loop, not a testimonial.

Import

Head office uploads the 2019 safeguarding PDF. That is the starting file. Nothing is generated from a blank page.

Overhaul

The draft is not fit. An overhaul rewrites it against current guidance. The original stays in versions.

Plan, then execute

The improvement plan still wants a night-shift appendix and a shorter escalation box. The manager accepts those two and runs the plan. That cut is v2.

Review on a cycle

Ninety days later a review checks it still complies. Findings become v3. The live published file does not change until someone signs it off.

Publish and distribute

v3 is published, classified Internal, and sent to employees at all four homes. Acknowledgements are per site.
