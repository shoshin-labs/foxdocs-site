Harbor Care Group

One register. Three homes. A safeguarding policy that has to be read.

This is a worked example of a multi-site rollout. Harbor Care does not exist. The scores, staff counts, and proposals below are illustrative, so you can see how the product loop holds a shared policy.

The group

Three sites, one policy owner at group.

A nominated lead at group holds the register. Each home has a manager and a staff list on Employees, not as extra app seats.

02 Improvement plan

Four proposals. Three accepted. One rejected.

Proposal Decision
Replace the outdated referral number and add the current duty desk path. Accepted
Add a short section for agency and bank staff who move between homes. Accepted
Rewrite the escalation appendix into numbered steps. Accepted
Insert a group-wide tone guide in the opening. Rejected

Rejected because the opening already points at the group style guide. The plan does not force the extra paragraph.

03 Publish

Version 2, classified, on the timeline.

The accepted proposals save as version 2. Group publishes it. The Timeline shows version created, then version published. Shares stay with the policy lead and the three site managers. Export can pull a branded PDF for the inspector’s pack, and Excel if they want the change report.

04 Distribute

One published file. Three distributions.

Employees at each home are on the staff register. They are not extra Users. Each site gets its own distribution of the same version 2, so acknowledgements can be read by home.

Distribution Who What you would look for
Seaview House Site manager, day team, night team Acknowledgements against version 2
Oak Lodge Site manager and staff list Same version, separate list
The Willows Site manager, day services, bank names from CSV Agency staff included on Employees

If group publishes version 3 later, signatures can be invalidated on the timeline. Then you distribute again. That is the point of the loop: the file in the homes matches the file in the register.

Again

This is an example, not a case study.

Harbor Care, the three homes, the scores, and the proposal list were written for this page. They are not a live customer. If you want the same shape for a real group, book a demo and bring one policy.
