The loop

One path from draft to acknowledgement.

Review names what is wrong. A plan lets you accept or reject each fix. An overhaul rewrites the whole document when the draft is too far gone. Then you publish, classify, and send it to staff.

Read the full loop, with the words the app actually uses: Versions, Timeline, Distributions, Shares, Export, Activity.

  1. 01

    Review

    Four sub-agents score the document: Fidelity, Completeness, Mechanics, Style. You get a mark, not a vibe.

  2. 02

    Improvement plan

    Scope the change, then accept or reject proposals one by one. Nothing lands without you.

  3. 03

    Overhaul

    A full-document rewrite against the document type template, when line edits will not save it.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Versions, a timeline, classification, and a branded export for the file.

  5. 05

    Distribute

    Employees are a separate register from app users. Acknowledgements match the people who have to read it.
