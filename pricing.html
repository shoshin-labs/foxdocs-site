The tariff

Same product. Four scopes.

Prices are monthly, in pounds. There is no annual discount on this page because we are not showing one. Seats are app users. Staff are employees on the acknowledgement register.

Monthly tariff · Foxdocs Limited
Tier Price Scope
Starter £65 / month 1 seat. Reviews, restructuring, chat to edit, current regulations.
Growth £145 / month 5 seats, 30 staff. Everything in Starter, plus staff notifications.
Business £475 / month 10 seats, 150 staff. Horizon scanning, custom knowledge base, Policy Co-Pilot, staff acceptance tracking.
Enterprise Talk to sales Unlimited seats and staff. SSO, bring your own keys, self-host, integrate anywhere. Book a conversation.

Starter

For a single policy owner. One seat, no staff register.

  • AI-powered reviews
  • Automated document restructuring
  • Chat to edit
  • Latest industry regulations
  • 1 seat

Growth

Small teams that need the staff list as well as the desk.

  • Everything in Starter
  • Staff notifications
  • Up to 5 seats
  • Up to 30 staff accounts

Business

The flagship scope. Complex regs, a knowledge base you own, and a co-pilot for staff who have to act on the policy.

  • Everything in Growth
  • Custom knowledge base
  • Regulation horizon scanning
  • Staff Policy Co-Pilot
  • Staff acceptance tracking
  • Up to 10 seats and 150 staff accounts

Enterprise

Built for scale and for the way your security team already works. Pricing is by arrangement.

  • Everything in Business
  • SSO
  • Integrate anywhere
  • Bring your own keys
  • Self-host
  • Unlimited seats and staff accounts
